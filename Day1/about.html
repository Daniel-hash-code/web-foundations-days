About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool for capturing and organizing your ideas. It helps you keep track of your thoughts, tasks and reminders in one convenient

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Visit the homepage
  2. Type your idea into the note input box
  3. Click the "Add note" button to save the note
  4. View your saved notes in the "Your notes" section
